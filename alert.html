Stay Safe Online

Simple guides to help you recognize scams and protect your personal information.

!
Urgent Alert: Fake Text Messages Claiming "Account Suspension"

Scammers are sending messages pretending to be banks or government agencies. They may ask you to click a link or provide personal information. Do NOT click suspicious links.

Learn what to do
✓
Real Email

Example: Official bank email about your monthly statement or receipt.

✔ Uses official company email (e.g. @bankname.com)
✔ No urgent threats or pressure
✔ Proper grammar and clear message
✔ Does NOT ask for passwords

Trusted Source
✕
Fake / Scam Email

Example: “Your account will be locked unless you verify immediately!”

  • Suspicious or shortened links
  • Urgent threats like “act now” or “within 24 hours”
  • Requests for passwords or OTP codes
  • Unknown or fake email address
  • Poor grammar and spelling mistakes
Use Strong Passwords

Create long passwords with letters, numbers, and symbols. Do not reuse the same password for different accounts.

Verify the Sender

Always check who is contacting you. If unsure, call the official hotline of the company or bank directly.

Check Secure Websites

Make sure the website starts with “https://” and has a padlock icon before entering personal details.
